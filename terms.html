Smoked meat is made in batches and some items can sell out. If something you ordered is not available, we will tell you and you can choose a replacement or remove it from your order.

3. How an order works

Building an order on the website and tapping "Send order on WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with your order typed out. Your order is an offer to buy. A contract is formed only when we reply on WhatsApp to confirm your order. We may decline an order, for example if we are sold out or closed, or if the delivery address is outside our area.

When we confirm, we will give you an estimated time for collection or delivery. You can pay by cash, card or EFT on collection or delivery.

4. Collection and delivery

You can collect from 14 Swartbos Avenue, Protea Park, or ask for delivery. We deliver only within Rustenburg. Delivery starts at R35. The fee depends on how far you are from us, so it may be higher for longer distances. The website shows an estimated distance as a guide only. We will confirm the final delivery fee on WhatsApp before we start preparing your order, and you can cancel at no cost if you do not accept it.

Collection and delivery times are good-faith estimates. Busy periods, weather and traffic can cause delays, and we will let you know on WhatsApp if we are running late.

Your responsibility: please give a complete, correct address and keep your phone nearby. If our driver cannot reach you or find the address after reasonable attempts, we may treat the order as delivered and you may need to pay for it. If an order is not collected within a reasonable time after it is ready, the same applies, because cooked food cannot be resold.

5. Changes, cancellations and refunds

You can change or cancel your order at no cost until we start preparing it. Once cooking has started, we may not be able to change or cancel it, because food is made to order and cannot be resold. The cooling-off period in section 44 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act does not apply to food and drinks supplied for everyday consumption.

If something is wrong with your order, for example a missing item, the wrong dish, or food that is not of good quality or not safe, tell us on WhatsApp as soon as possible and ideally within 2 hours, with a photo if you can. We will replace the item or refund you for it, as the Consumer Protection Act requires. This does not limit any other rights you have under that Act.

5A. Pre-orders and food sold by weight

Some items, such as our whole piece brisket, are made to order and must be ordered at least 24 hours ahead. They are priced per kilogram. The weight you choose on the website is an estimate: the final price is worked out from the actual weight of your piece and confirmed on WhatsApp.

Deposit and cancelling a pre-order: a pre-order is only confirmed once you have paid a 50% deposit, based on the estimated price. We send payment details on WhatsApp. A whole brisket goes on the smoker for about 18 hours as soon as your deposit is received, so the deposit is not refundable if you cancel after that, or if you do not collect or accept delivery at the agreed time, because the brisket cannot be resold. You can cancel free of charge before you pay the deposit. If we cannot supply your pre-order for any reason, we refund your deposit in full. The balance is due on collection or delivery, based on the actual weight.

Important, please read: our kitchen handles milk, wheat (gluten), eggs, soy and other common allergens, and our dishes are made in a shared kitchen and on shared equipment. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free of any allergen, including traces of nuts. If you or anyone you are ordering for has a food allergy or intolerance, tell us in your order notes and ask us about the ingredients before ordering. If we are not sure a dish is safe for you, we will tell you and may decline to make it.

Our menu is described as "mild" where a dish is not spicy, but taste is personal. Ask us if you are unsure.
