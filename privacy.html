Privacy notice

Last updated 20 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information The Smoke Shack collects through this website and when you order from us, why we collect it and what we do with it. We follow the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who we are

The responsible party is The Smoke Shack (The Smoke Shack Texas Smokeroom), founded and run by Dandre Venter, 14 Swartbos Avenue, Protea Park, Rustenburg, North West, South Africa.

Our Information Officer is Dandre Venter. You can reach him on WhatsApp or phone at 060 537 5032, or by email at smokeshack@gmail.com.

Giving us your name, phone number and (for delivery) your address is voluntary, but we cannot take or deliver your order without them.

3. How ordering works

The order form on this website does not send your details to us directly and we do not store them on a server. When you tap "Send order on WhatsApp", the form opens WhatsApp on your device with your order typed out. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. From that moment your message is handled by WhatsApp (owned by Meta) under WhatsApp's own privacy policy, which may involve servers outside South Africa.

While you build an order, the items in your tray are saved in your own browser so they survive a page refresh. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies to track you. We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits and taps on buttons such as "Add" and "Send order". Umami does not use cookies, does not identify you personally and stores its data on servers in the European Union. We see totals, not individuals.

Our website is hosted by Vercel, which keeps short-term technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service. These may be processed outside South Africa.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. We only share it where needed to run the business: with WhatsApp (Meta) as the messaging channel you chose, with our hosting and analytics providers as described above, with anyone delivering your order on our behalf (only your name, address and phone number), and with authorities where the law requires it.

Where information is processed outside South Africa, we use providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements that give protection similar to POPIA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your order details only as long as we need them to fulfil your order, deal with any questions or complaints, honour vouchers and meet legal record-keeping duties, such as tax records. After that we delete them or make them anonymous. You can delete our WhatsApp chat on your side at any time.

7. Marketing

We will not send you marketing messages unless you have asked to receive them. If you do opt in, you can stop them at any time by replying "STOP" or telling us on WhatsApp.

8. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting who in the business can see order messages and keeping our devices locked. If we ever believe your information has been accessed by someone who should not have it, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

9. Your rights

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to us using it, and to withdraw any consent you gave. Contact our Information Officer using the details in section 1. We will answer within a reasonable time and will not charge you for a simple request.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Children

Our kids menu is for children, but orders should be placed by an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

11. Changes

We may update this notice when the way we work changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
