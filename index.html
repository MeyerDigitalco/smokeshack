The pit and the man behind it

No shortcuts.
Just smoke and time.

The Smoke Shack is Dandre Venter's love letter to Texas barbecue, built right here in Rustenburg. Brisket gets a heavy pepper rub and a long, slow ride in the smoker until the bark goes dark and the inside goes tender. Pork gets the same patience before it's pulled by hand.

Then we have a little fun with it. That smoked meat ends up in Portuguese rolls under a blanket of cheese, wrapped in naan with tzatziki, or piled on nachos and hand cut slap chips. Texas technique, South African appetite.
